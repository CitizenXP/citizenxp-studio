Settings

Event setup

Room names may only contain letters, numbers and underscores. Both rooms share one password (required so guests can be moved between them).

Guest features & audio

Changes bake into the links, so re-copy the guest invite and OBS links after saving. Guests already connected keep their old settings until they rejoin.

Broadcast & recording

A guest records their camera exactly as it was captured, so this is the ceiling on your masters. Auto lets VDO.Ninja guess from the guest's hardware, and on an older laptop that guess can be as low as 320x180. Re-copy the guest links after changing this.

Shown to each guest the moment their recording stops, as a button they can click. A Dropbox File Request works best: guests can upload without an account. Google Drive folders usually force them to sign in.

Recordings save to this computer's downloads folder. Standby settings apply to newly copied OBS links.
